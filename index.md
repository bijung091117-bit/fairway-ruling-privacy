# Fairway Ruling Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: September 3, 2026**

Fairway Ruling (“Fairway,” “we,” “our,” or “the app”) is a golf rules assistance application that provides conversational rules guidance, golf course map tools, and visual relief assistance.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use Fairway Ruling and how that information is used.

## 1. Information We Process

### Location Information

Fairway Ruling may request access to your device’s location while you are using the app.

Location may be used to:

- show your approximate position on a golf course;
- identify nearby golf courses;
- display course and map information;
- support map-based ruling and relief visualization.

Fairway Ruling does not require continuous background location access.

You may disable location access at any time through your device settings, although some map and nearby-course features may not function properly without it.

### Information You Provide

When using Fairway Ruling, you may voluntarily provide information such as:

- descriptions of golf situations;
- ball status and approximate location;
- penalty area or out-of-bounds information;
- map points, boundaries, crossing points, and estimated positions;
- applicable Local Rules;
- other golf-related information entered into the app.

This information is used to provide rules guidance, relief options, and visual ruling assistance.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to:

- answer golf rules questions;
- determine relevant ruling and relief options;
- provide map-based visual guidance;
- identify nearby golf courses;
- improve reliability, security, and troubleshooting of the service.

Fairway Ruling does not use your location for advertising.

Fairway Ruling does not sell your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

## 3. How Information Is Processed

Fairway Ruling may transmit information necessary to provide its features to its backend service and third-party service providers.

For example:

- rules questions and relevant ruling context may be processed by an artificial intelligence service;
- geographic coordinates may be used to obtain golf course and map information;
- application requests may pass through cloud hosting infrastructure.

Only information reasonably necessary to perform the requested feature is intended to be transmitted.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Fairway Ruling currently uses third-party services that may include:

- **OpenAI** for artificial intelligence processing;
- **Render** for backend application hosting;
- **Overspan** for OpenStreetMap-related course and geographic data services;
- **OpenStreetMap** data for map and golf course information.

These providers may process information transmitted to them according to their own privacy policies, terms, and data-processing practices.

Fairway Ruling is not responsible for the independent privacy practices of third-party service providers.

## 5. Location Data

Fairway Ruling requests location access only when location-based functionality is used.

Location information may be used to determine your approximate position, locate nearby golf courses, and support visual ruling features.

Fairway Ruling does not intentionally use precise location data to create an advertising profile or track users for advertising purposes.

## 6. Data Retention

Fairway Ruling does not currently require users to create an account and does not currently maintain a persistent personal golf profile associated with a user account.

Information submitted to provide rules or mapping functionality may be processed temporarily by Fairway Ruling’s backend infrastructure and third-party service providers.

Operational, diagnostic, and security logs may be retained for a limited period when reasonably necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, or improve the service.

Third-party service providers may retain information according to their own policies.

## 7. Data Deletion and Privacy Requests

Because Fairway Ruling currently does not provide user accounts or persistent cloud profiles, there may be limited user-associated information stored directly by Fairway Ruling.

If you believe information associated with you has been retained and would like to request access, correction, or deletion, please contact:

**fairwayruling.app@gmail.com**

We will review reasonable privacy requests and respond as required by applicable law.

## 8. Children’s Privacy

Fairway Ruling is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we learn that personal information from a child has been collected inappropriately, we will take reasonable steps to delete it where applicable.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information transmitted through Fairway Ruling.

However, no internet transmission, cloud service, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Fairway Ruling evolves.

If material changes are made, the updated Privacy Policy will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
